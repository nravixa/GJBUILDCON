## 2. CREATIVE DIRECTOR'S FORMAL RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Hero Archetype:** Recommend **Direction 03 ("The Tectonic Split")** or **Direction 01 ("The Monumental Monolith")**. Direction 03 provides the most disciplined architectural tension and easiest multi-page scaling; Direction 01 provides the most immediate typographic impact.
2. **Color Palette:** Strongly recommend **Palette Alpha ("Travertine & Deccan Basalt")**. It delivers the exact warm, unbleached architectural paper quality requested by the creative brief while providing high-contrast depth in the piling section.
3. **Typography System:** Strongly recommend **System A (Three-Font System)**. *Instrument Serif* provides an irreplaceable sense of structural heritage, while *Inter* guarantees immaculate tabular readability and *Space Mono* anchors equipment specifications.
4. **Media Strategy:** Strongly recommend **Option 01 (Still Imagery with Scroll-Driven Crop Expansion)**. GJ Buildcon has zero authentic video footage; using still photography guarantees instant loading (<0.8s) and flawless mobile responsiveness.
5. **Section Choreography:** Strongly recommend **Treatment A (Warm Monograph Base with Selective Dark Piling Inversion)**. Maintains a calm, cohesive reading experience with dramatic contrast at the narrative climax.
