## 1. Executive Summary & Confirmed Owner Decisions

Prior to commercial launch, a systematic content-risk cleanup was conducted across the GJ Buildcon web application to neutralize unverified public claims, prevent intellectual property misrepresentation, and document all outstanding evidentiary prerequisites.

### Confirmed Owner Decisions Matrix
1. **Partner Permissions: NOT CONFIRMED**  
   *Decision:* Written consent and trademark usage rights have not yet been established for third-party developer brands. Public wording asserting verified client relationships, endorsements, and commercial brand marks must be removed or neutralized into regional industry typologies until written agreements or MOUs are verified.
2. **Fleet Ownership: NEEDS CONFIRMATION**  
   *Decision:* Whether the heavy machinery (MAIT and SANY rotary rigs) is legally owned by *G and J Buildcon*, owned personally by *Mr. Umesh Gatkal*, operated under commercial hire-purchase/lease, or contracted via dedicated logistics arrangements has not been confirmed with title deeds or registration certificates. All assertions of "100% In-House Fleet", "Direct Fleet Ownership", and specific unit breakdowns ("4 MAIT + 2 SANY") must be neutralized to capability-based phrasing ("Drilling Equipment & Fleet Capabilities").
3. **Real Company & Project Photographs: ADD AFTER LAUNCH**  
   *Decision:* Real site photography will be captured and cleared post-launch. In the interim, conceptual and representative assets are retained strictly with transparent disclosure badges (*"Architectural Concept Study // Demo Asset"*, *"Representative Fleet Visual · Demo Asset"*). No stock photos may be claimed as completed GJ Buildcon structures or company-owned rigs.
4. **Numerical Claims: MUST BE VERIFIED BEFORE LAUNCH**  
   *Decision:* Uncorroborated numerical statistics (*"25+ Lakh Sq. Ft."*, *"700+ Skilled Manpower"*, *"06 Heavy Rigs"*) cannot be published as verified guarantees without supporting documentary evidence (completion certificates, muster rolls, registration documents). All figures must be replaced with accurate, qualitative capability descriptions.
5. **Deployment & DNS Changes: NOT AUTHORIZED**  
   *Decision:* Production deployment, Vercel project attachment, DNS cutover, and external transactional services remain strictly unauthorized during Phase 3D.
